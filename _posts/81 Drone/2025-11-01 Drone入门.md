## 5 参考资料 ##

Top 3 Video Courses on Photogrammetry, SfM, MVS & Drone 3D Reconstruction
Based on popularity, ratings, comprehensiveness, and relevance to WebODM-style workflows (drone image processing for 3D models). Practical, hands-on courses with video lectures.

1.  **Drone Photogrammetric Image Processing (Beginner to Expert)** (Udemy)
    - *Key Highlights:* Covers SfM/MVS end-to-end: aerial triangulation, orthos, point clouds, DSM/DTM, 3D meshes; RTK/PPK processing; 13+ years instructor experience in drone/GIS projects. (13 hours, 4.6/5 rating)
    - *Link:* Udemy Link

2.  **Introduction to 2 & 3D Drone Modeling With Agisoft Metashape** (Udemy)
    - *Key Highlights:* Focuses on drone flight setups, SfM for 2D/3D models; hands-on with Metashape (similar to ODM); ideal for beginners building textured models. (4.5 hours, 4.5/5 rating)
    - *Link:* Udemy Link

3.  **EN.1. UAV Drones: Introduction to 3D Mapping** (Udemy)
    - *Key Highlights:* Photogrammetry basics, SfM applications in industries, equipment/software (incl. ODM-like tools), business models; safety tips for drone surveys. (2 hours, 4.4/5 rating)
    - *Link:* Udemy Link

- University Open Course Bonus: For a free, academic option, check MIT OpenCourseWare's "Shape, Form, and Architecture" (includes SfM/photogrammetry modules with videos/lectures): MIT OCW Link.

Top 3 Free Online Books on Photogrammetry, SfM, & Drone 3D Reconstruction
Accessible PDFs/e-books focusing on theory (SfM/MVS) and practical drone applications.

1.  **Introduction to Photogrammetry** (T. Schenk, Ohio State University)
    - *Key Highlights:* Comprehensive intro to cameras, SfM, bundle adjustment, stereo plotting; university-level textbook with exercises. (Free PDF, 200+ pages)
    - *Link:* PDF Link

2.  **Structure-from-Motion Photogrammetry: A Low-Cost, Effective Tool for Geoscience Applications** (James S. Aber et al.)
    - *Key Highlights:* Practical SfM guide for geomorphology/drone mapping; low-cost workflows, examples with overlapping images. (Free PDF, 20+ pages)
    - *Link:* PDF Link

3.  **UAV Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing** (Juan Manuel Delgado, MDPI Books)
    - *Key Highlights:* Drone-specific: SfM/MVS for point clouds, orthos, 3D models; open-access with case studies in surveying/terrain. (Free PDF, 200+ pages)
    - *Link:* PDF Link

Note: These resources align closely with WebODM's pipeline—start with videos for hands-on practice, then dive into books for theory.
